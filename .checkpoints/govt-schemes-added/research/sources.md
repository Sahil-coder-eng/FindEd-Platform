## Data Collection Methodology

1. Interest rates, processing fees, and loan terms were noted from each lender's
   official public-facing website.
2. Where exact current rates were behind application forms or required profile-based
   quotation, approximate ranges were recorded and noted accordingly.
3. Fields that could not be confirmed from the public source were marked as
   "Not Available" rather than assumed to be zero.
4. All entries include the date of last verification.

## Data Limitations

- Rates change periodically based on RBI policy, lender decisions, and borrower profile.
- Some lenders require an application to provide exact rates — publicly visible rates
  may be indicative ranges.
- Processing fees and other charges may have promotional waivers.
- Moratorium terms may vary by specific loan product within the same lender.
