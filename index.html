How EMI is Calculated

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is calculated using the standard reducing-balance formula:

EMI = P × r × (1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1)

Where P = Principal, r = Monthly interest rate (annual/12/100), n = Total months (years × 12).

Each EMI payment goes partly toward interest and partly toward reducing your principal. In the early months, a larger portion goes to interest; over time, more goes toward principal repayment.

About This Platform

🎯 Purpose

This is a transparent education-financing decision-support platform designed to help students and parents understand the true cost of education loans.

We are not simply an EMI calculator. This platform brings together:

  • EMI calculation with full mathematical transparency
  • Multi-lender comparison across documented attributes
  • Repayment schedule visualization
  • Moratorium period impact analysis
  • Total cost estimation including applicable charges
  • Scenario and sensitivity analysis
  • International education cost planning
  • Relevant destination updates

🔬 Methodology

  1. Problem Identification → Evidence from credible sources
  2. Literature Review → Academic papers, existing platforms
  3. Research Gap → What existing solutions don't provide
  4. Mathematical Model → Standard reducing-balance EMI formula
  5. System Architecture → Modular calculation + presentation layers
  6. Prototype Development → Working web application
  7. Validation → Unit tests + cross-validation with known calculators

🎯 SDG Alignment

  • SDG 4 — Quality Education: Supporting informed access to education financing for higher/tertiary education.
  • SDG 10 — Reduced Inequalities: Improving access to understandable financial information for education planning.
